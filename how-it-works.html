Step-by-Step Clarity

The 5-Step Publishing Lifecycle

We eliminate ambiguity with clear milestones, rapid turnaround windows, and structured editorial reviews.

1

Choose Your Package

Select the tier that aligns with your desired depth and scope: Starter ($500) for up to 8k words, Professional ($900) for up to 20k words, or Premium ($1500+) for up to 40k words. Choose standard or fast delivery speed.

2

Submit Project Brief

Complete our structured intake form on order.html. Provide your thesis, target reader persona, tone preferences, and any notes or recordings you have prepared.

3

Pay Securely via Stripe

Following brief review, we send you an official Stripe invoice, or you can pay via direct payment link. Payment is processed with 256-bit SSL encryption, locking in your production window.

4

Drafting & Revisions

Our writers produce your complete original manuscript and design your custom cover. You receive the draft for thorough review and request revisions according to your package tier.

5

Final Master Delivery

You receive production-grade PDF and EPUB master files. 100% intellectual property rights and commercial copyright transfer to you. Optional physical print copies can be produced via US partners.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers regarding ownership, turnaround times, revisions, and fulfillment.

Turnaround times depend on your chosen package and selected delivery speed:
  • Starter ($500): 7 business days standard (or 4 days fast rush for +$150).
  • Professional ($900): 12 business days standard (or 7 days fast rush for +$250).
  • Premium ($1500+): 20 business days standard (or 12 days fast rush for +$400).
Ready-made e-books from our store are delivered instantly upon payment completion.

You are recognized as the sole author and copyright holder. You may sell, distribute, repurpose, or monetize the manuscript anywhere in the world without paying royalties.
Each package includes dedicated revision cycles:
  • Starter: 2 rounds of editorial revisions.
  • Professional: 4 rounds of comprehensive revisions.
  • Premium: Unlimited revisions within the 14-day post-delivery review window.
Revisions cover rewording, thematic adjustments, tone calibrations, and layout formatting refinements.

For custom projects, clients may request a 100% refund prior to the commencement of research and outline drafting.
