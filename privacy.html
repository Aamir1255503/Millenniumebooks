Legal Documentation

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Last Updated: September 2026

Home / Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy outlines how Nova Forge LLC ("we", "our", or "us"), operating the brand and website Millenniumebooks (www.millenniumebooks.com), collects, uses, protects, and discloses personal information obtained from visitors, clients, and purchasers.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect information necessary to deliver our custom publishing services and digital book downloads:

  • Direct Contact Details: Name, business name, and email address provided via contact forms, newsletter signups, or custom project brief submissions.
  • Project Specifications: Topic details, manuscript outlines, target audience personas, and background notes submitted through order.html.
  • Order & Transaction Records: Transaction IDs, purchase dates, selected package tiers, and purchased e-book titles.
  • Technical Usage Data: Standard server logs, IP address, browser type, and anonymous aggregate page engagement metrics to maintain site speed and security.

2. Payment Information & Stripe Security

We do not collect, process, or store full credit card numbers or banking credentials on our servers. All financial transactions are processed directly through Stripe, Inc. (stripe.com), a PCI-DSS Level 1 certified payment processor.

When you purchase an e-book or pay a custom invoice, your payment details are transmitted using end-to-end 256-bit SSL encryption directly to Stripe. We receive only non-sensitive confirmation details (such as the last four digits of your card, billing name, and confirmation status).

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal data strictly for lawful business purposes:

  • To fulfill and deliver digital e-book downloads (PDF/EPUB master files) immediately upon purchase.
  • To research, ghostwrite, format, and deliver bespoke custom e-book commissions.
  • To issue invoices, process receipts, and manage project revisions.
  • To respond to customer support inquiries and provide transactional notifications.
  • To send occasional editorial announcements or new title releases if you explicitly subscribed to our newsletter.

4. Data Sharing and Third-Party Providers

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We share data only with trusted service providers necessary for operating our platform:

  • Payment Processing: Stripe, Inc.
  • Form Processing: Formspree / Web3Forms for secure form transmission.
  • Print-on-Demand Partners: If you explicitly request physical print copies, shipping address details are shared solely with our US print fulfillment partner to execute shipment.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information if required to do so by applicable US law, subpoena, or regulatory request.

5. Intellectual Property & Confidentiality

All manuscript briefs, outlines, and client notes submitted for custom ghostwriting are treated under strict confidentiality. We will never share, publish, or recycle your proprietary concepts or unreleased drafts without your prior written authorization.

6. Cookies & Tracking

Millenniumebooks utilizes minimal functional cookies required for site navigation, security verification, and caching. We do not engage in invasive behavioral tracking or third-party ad retargeting networks. You may configure your browser to block cookies, though certain site features may function with reduced performance.

7. Data Retention & Your Rights

We retain order history and correspondence records for as long as necessary to provide service, comply with legal accounting obligations, and resolve disputes. You have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you, request corrections, or request deletion of your contact records.
