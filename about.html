Who We Are

Dedicated to Meaningful Digital Literature

Millenniumebooks is the premier digital publishing imprint of Nova Forge LLC, a United States limited liability company.

The digital reading landscape has become flooded with recycled articles, generic PLR bundles, and unverified summaries. We built Millenniumebooks as an antidote: a publishing house dedicated solely to deep, thoroughly researched, 100% original manuscripts.

Our business operates across two focused pillars: crafting bespoke custom books for leaders, founders, and consultants through our white-glove ghostwriting service, and publishing original standalone e-books across strategy, productivity, and technology through our digital storefront.

Our Editorial Commitment

Nova Forge LLC is committed to ethical publishing. When you purchase an e-book from our store, you receive a thoroughly edited, self-contained educational asset. When you hire us for custom ghostwriting, we respect your confidential voice, transferring 100% of the copyright directly to you upon project completion.

Print Fulfillment: While our workflows prioritize efficient digital delivery, we recognize that many authors and corporate clients value physical copies for conferences, executive desks, and client gifts. For this reason, we coordinate with specialized US print-on-demand partners whenever requested.
