Refund & Delivery Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Last Updated: September 2026

Home / Refund & Delivery Policy

This Refund & Delivery Policy governs the delivery protocols, turnaround timelines, and cancellation/refund criteria for all purchases made through Millenniumebooks (www.millenniumebooks.com), operated by Nova Forge LLC.

1. Digital Product Delivery (Ready-Made Store)

All ready-made e-books sold in our store ($100–$300) are purely digital products delivered in standard, uncompressed master formats: Universal PDF and Reflowable EPUB.

  • Delivery Method: Immediately upon successful Stripe payment confirmation, you are redirected to a secure download page with direct links. Additionally, an automated email receipt containing persistent download links is dispatched to your provided billing email address.
  • Delivery Timeframe: Instantaneous (under 60 seconds). If you do not observe your confirmation email within 5 minutes, please inspect your spam/promotions folder or email us at info@millenniumebooks.com with your Stripe transaction ID.

2. Refund Policy for Ready-Made Digital E-Books

Due to the irrevocable digital nature of instantly downloaded e-books (PDF and EPUB files cannot be technically "returned" once accessed), our policy for ready-made books is as follows:

  • Defective Files or Technical Incompatibility: If a downloaded file is corrupted, unreadable, or missing pages, we will immediately provide verified replacement files or alternative formatting within 24 hours. If technical issues persist, we will issue a 100% refund.
  • Duplicate Transactions: If your card was erroneously charged twice for the same title due to a network lag or browser refresh, we will immediately refund the duplicate payment upon notification.
  • General Dissatisfaction: Because digital files are delivered immediately upon purchase, general refunds requested after files have been successfully downloaded are evaluated on a case-by-case basis. Please contact info@millenniumebooks.com within 7 days of purchase explaining your concern.

3. Delivery Protocols for Custom Ghostwriting Projects

Custom ghostwriting and packaging services operate according to agreed-upon production schedules:

  • Starter Package ($500): Standard delivery in 7 business days (Fast rush option: 4 business days for +$150).
  • Professional Package ($900): Standard delivery in 12 business days (Fast rush option: 7 business days for +$250).
  • Premium Package ($1500+): Standard delivery in 20 business days (Fast rush option: 12 business days for +$400).

Delivery timelines commence the business day following receipt of the client's completed project brief and cleared payment. Drafts and final master files are delivered via secure private cloud download links sent to the client's verified email.

4. Cancellation & Refund Policy for Custom Projects

Custom ghostwriting requires substantial editorial labor, research, and dedicated writer calendar booking. Cancellations are handled through the following structured milestones:

  • Phase 1 — Prior to Work Commencement (Full Refund): If you cancel your custom project in writing within 24 hours of payment and before our editorial desk has commenced outlining and research, you are entitled to a 100% full refund.
  • Phase 2 — During Outline & Initial Research Stage (75% Refund): If cancellation is requested after the structural outline has been drafted but before full manuscript writing begins, a 75% refund is issued (25% retained for editorial research and outline preparation).
  • Phase 3 — During Full Manuscript Drafting (50% Refund): If cancellation is requested while primary drafting is actively underway and before delivery of the first full draft, a 50% refund is issued to compensate for writer hours invested.
  • Phase 4 — Post-Draft Delivery & Completed Works (Non-Refundable): Once the full manuscript draft has been delivered to the client for editorial review, the project fee is non-refundable. At this stage, our commitment is fulfilled through the allotted revision rounds included with your package (2 rounds for Starter, 4 for Professional, unlimited for Premium).

5. Optional Physical Print-on-Demand Copies

Primary deliverables for all custom commissions are digital master files (PDF & EPUB). If physical print copies are coordinated via our US print-on-demand fulfillment partners:

  • Print copies are custom-manufactured on-demand and are non-refundable once sent to press.
  • In the event of shipping damage, misprints, or binding defects caused by the print partner, photographic documentation must be emailed to us within 10 days of delivery, and replacement copies will be re-printed and dispatched at no additional charge.

6. Refund Processing Timelines

All approved refunds are submitted through Stripe to the original payment method utilized during checkout. Stripe typically credits funds back to the cardholder's bank statement within 3 to 5 business days, depending on your financial institution's processing cadence.
